### Long Description (4,000 characters max)

**Introducing NutriTrack — Your Personal Nutrition Companion**

NutriTrack is a comprehensive nutrition tracking app designed for anyone looking to take control of their health through informed eating habits. Whether you're focused on building muscle, losing weight, or simply maintaining a balanced diet, NutriTrack makes macro and calorie tracking effortless.

**Core Features:**

**Food Logging & Nutrition Tracking**
- Access to 751 USDA-certified foods with complete nutritional data
- Search by name in English or Turkish for instant results
- Log food intake in multiple units: grams, milliliters, pieces, slices, or servings
- Automatic calorie calculation based on macronutrient values
- Custom food library to track your favorite meals and recipes

**Macro & Calorie Tracking**
- Set personalized daily targets for protein, fat, carbohydrates, and calories
- Visual macro donut chart showing real-time progress toward your goals
- Daily summary cards displaying calories consumed and macros achieved
- Historical tracking to identify patterns and trends

**Meal Templates for One-Tap Logging**
- Save your favorite meal combinations as templates
- Premium users get unlimited templates; free users get up to 5
- Edit template portions on-the-fly before logging
- Perfect for breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snacks you eat regularly

**Water & Wellness Tracking**
- Log daily water intake to stay hydrated
- Daily notes feature for recording mood, energy levels, or observations
- All-in-one view of your complete nutrition and wellness picture

**Social & Family Mode**
- Add friends and family members to your network
- Share your nutrition data with others or keep it private
- Compete in weekly leaderboards with customizable scoring
- Earn achievement badges for consistency and milestones
- Feed visibility controls let you choose exactly what to share

**Advanced Features:**
- Offline-first architecture works without internet connection
- Turkish and English language support
- Responsive design for phones, tablets, and responsive web browsers
- Built-in AI chat assistant (optional—works offline with local NLP, or bring your own Claude API key for enhanced features)
- CSV export of your nutrition history (Premium only)
- Advanced analytics and monthly/weekly reports (Premium only)
- Dietitian-assigned nutrition plans (for users with plan access)

**Why Choose NutriTrack?**

✓ **Completely Free** — No ads, no paywalls on core features (free tier never expires)  
✓ **Privacy First** — Your data is yours. All data encrypted in transit and at rest  
✓ **Local-First** — Use offline anytime; sync automatically when online  
✓ **No Medical Claims** — We're a lifestyle app, not a medical device  
✓ **Turkish + English** — Full multilingual support for both languages  
✓ **Open Source Friendly** — Built with open-source technologies  

**Premium Membership (Coming Soon)**
Unlock unlimited food history, unlimited templates, family leaderboards, CSV export, and advanced reports. Estimated pricing: $2.99/month or $19.99/year.

**Medical Disclaimer:**
NutriTrack is a personal nutrition tracking tool and is NOT medical advice or a medical device. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant dietary changes. NutriTrack creators are not responsible for health outcomes from using this app.

**Privacy & Security:**
Your data is stored on Neon Postgres (Google Cloud infrastructure) with SSL/TLS encryption. You own your data and can request deletion anytime via account settings. We never sell your data to advertisers or third parties.

**Get Started Today:**
Download NutriTrack and start your nutrition journey with the app trusted by health-conscious users worldwide. Track smarter, live healthier.
